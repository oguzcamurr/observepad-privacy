Common questions

How are interval percentages calculated? For partial and whole interval recording, only intervals that were observed in full count. The percentage is the number of intervals you marked divided by the number of complete intervals. For momentary time sampling, a sample counts once its signal has been reached.

What happens if I leave the app during an observation? The clock pauses, so time you didn't observe isn't counted. Tap Resume to continue.

What if the app closes unexpectedly? Every tap is saved instantly, and the clock position is saved every few seconds. The next time you open ObservePad, the observation is recovered and paused at the last saved moment.

Why don't I hear the signal? Every signal vibrates. Sounds are optional (Settings) and follow the ring/silent switch.

Can I fix mistakes? Yes. After finishing, adjust counts, add or delete duration episodes, edit ABC entries and correct interval marks on the summary screen.

I bought Pro on another device. Open Settings → Restore Purchase.

Does ObservePad sync or back up to a server? No. Data stays on your iPhone. It can be included in your own iPhone backups.
