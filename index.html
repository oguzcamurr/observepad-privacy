ObservePad — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · App: ObservePad: Behavior Data (iOS) · Developer: Oguz Camur · Contact: oguzcamuur@outlook.com

Summary

ObservePad does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your observations.

What ObservePad stores on your device

  • Student codes. The codes or initials you enter to identify students. The app asks you to use codes or initials rather than names.
  • Observations. The behaviors, counts, durations, interval marks, ABC entries, setting, observer initials and notes you record.
  • Preferences. Settings such as the signal sound, app lock and report paper size.

This information is stored only in ObservePad's private storage on your iPhone and is protected with iOS Data Protection, which keeps it encrypted while your iPhone is locked. You can turn on an optional Face ID or passcode lock in Settings. Like other app data, it can be included in backups of your iPhone that you make to iCloud or a computer.

Deleting data

You can delete a single observation, a student together with all of their observations, or all data (Settings → Delete All Data). Deleting the app also deletes its data. Deletion cannot be undone.

Exports

With ObservePad Pro you can create PDF reports and CSV spreadsheets. A report is created only when you ask for it and is handed to the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes (for example Files, AirDrop or Mail). Exported files contain the student code and observation data. Once shared, they are outside ObservePad's control, so store and share them only as your school or organization allows. Temporary export files are removed from the app on the next launch.

Purchases

The optional ObservePad Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment details or Apple Account information.

Your responsibilities

ObservePad is a tool for recording your own observations. You are responsible for following the laws and policies that apply to student records in your setting, including obtaining any required consent. ObservePad does not claim compliance with any specific education or health privacy law, and it does not diagnose, assess or interpret behavior.

Children

ObservePad is intended for adult professionals. It collects no data from anyone.
